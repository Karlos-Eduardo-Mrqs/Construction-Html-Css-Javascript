## Comparison Operators

Comparison operators are used to compare values and return a Boolean result (`true` or `false`).

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0bb2c93f-334e-45b4-b838-2afb01060fb0)

### Comparison Examples

1. 15 greater than (`>`) 9 ?: **true**
2. 15 less than (`<`) 9 ?: **false**
3. 15 greater than or equal to (`>=`) 9 ?: **true**
4. 15 less than or equal to (`<=`) 9 ?: **false**
5. 15 equal in value (`==`) 9 ?: **false**
6. 15 identical in value and type (`===`) 9 ?: **false**
7. 15 different in value (`!=`) 9 ?: **true**
8. 15 different in value or type (`!==`) 9 ?: **true**

> **Caption:** true = `true` and false = `false`.

---

## Logical Operators

Logical operators are used to combine or invert Boolean values. They are essential for evaluating complex conditions and making decisions based on the results.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b8e25dfe-1f02-4dc8-a947-c9553bae7dbf)

### Usage Examples

1. `8 > 10` **and** (`&&`) `13 < 10` ?: **false**
2. `8 > 9` **or** (`||`) `13 < 15` ?: **true**
3. `not` (`!`) `8 > 16` ?: **true**
4. `not` (`!`) `13 < 13` ?: **true**

> **Caption:** true = `true` and false = `false`.
